Security

How we protect Marketsphare accounts, payments and data, and how to report a vulnerability.

01Our approach

Trust is the foundation of a marketplace. We design Marketsphare with security and privacy in mind, apply the principle of least privilege, and continuously improve our controls as threats evolve.

02Protecting accounts

  • Identity verification: Talent and Employers complete identity checks so you know who you work with.
  • Strong authentication: passwords are stored using salted, industry-standard hashing, and we support additional sign-in protections.
  • Suspicious activity monitoring: automated and manual reviews flag unusual sign-ins and behaviour.

03Protecting payments

  • Escrow: funds are held securely and released only on approval or dispute resolution.
  • PCI-compliant partners: card and bank details are processed by certified payment providers, not stored on our servers.
  • Fraud controls: transaction screening and payout checks help prevent abuse.

04Protecting data

  • Encryption in transit using TLS and encryption at rest for sensitive data.
  • Role-based access controls, logging and regular access reviews for our team.
  • Backups and recovery procedures to protect against data loss.
  • Secure development practices, dependency monitoring and code review.

05Staying safe on Marketsphare

  • Keep all communication and payments on the Platform so you stay protected by escrow.
  • Never share your password or one-time codes. We will never ask for them.
  • Be cautious of offers that seem too good to be true, requests to move to other apps, or links to unfamiliar sites.
  • Use a unique password and keep your devices and browser up to date.
  • Report suspicious users or messages from the profile or through the contact page.

06Responsible disclosure

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please tell us privately so we can fix it before it is exploited. Email security@marketsphare.com with:

  • a clear description of the issue and its potential impact;
  • steps to reproduce, proof-of-concept or relevant logs;
  • your contact details if you would like updates.

Please act in good faith: avoid accessing or modifying other users' data, do not degrade the service, and give us reasonable time to respond before any public disclosure. We will acknowledge reports promptly and keep you informed of progress.

07Incident response

We maintain an incident response process to detect, contain and remediate security events. Where a breach affects personal data, we will notify affected users and regulators as required by law.
