Our Story

Built by marketers, for marketers

Marketsphare was founded in 2023 by a team of former agency leads and freelance marketers who were frustrated with clunky, generic freelance platforms that didn't understand the marketing industry.

Today, we power thousands of successful job placements and service engagements every month, combining AI-driven matching with the security and tools that serious professionals need.

  • Verified professionals and employers
  • Escrow-protected payments on every project
  • AI matching built for marketing roles
