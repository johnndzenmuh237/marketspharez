01What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help sites work properly, remember your preferences and understand how they are used. We also use similar technologies such as local storage and pixels, which we refer to together as "cookies".

02How we use cookies

TypePurposeCan you opt out?
Strictly necessaryLog you in, keep your session secure, balance traffic, protect against fraud, and store your cookie choiceNo (required for the Platform to work)
PreferencesRemember settings such as light or dark theme and languageYes
AnalyticsUnderstand how visitors use the Platform so we can improve it, using aggregated dataYes
MarketingMeasure the performance of campaigns and show relevant Marketsphare contentYes

04Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by trusted third parties, for example payment, analytics or font providers. These parties have their own privacy policies, and we do not control their cookies.

05Managing your preferences

  • Use the cookie banner on our site to accept all cookies or choose essential only.
  • Change your browser settings to block or delete cookies. Blocking necessary cookies may stop parts of the Platform working.
  • Clear site data in your browser to reset your choice; the banner will then appear again.

06Updates and contact

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Read how we handle personal data generally in our Privacy Policy.
