01Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Marketsphare ("we", "us") handles personal information when you visit our website or use our marketplace. We process personal data in line with the principles of lawfulness, fairness, transparency, purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation and security.

By using the Platform you acknowledge this Policy. If you disagree with it, please do not use our services.

03How we use your information

  • Provide the service: create accounts, publish profiles and jobs, enable messaging, process escrow and payouts.
  • Match and recommend: power AI matching between jobs and professionals.
  • Keep the Platform safe: verify identities, detect fraud and abuse, enforce our Terms.
  • Communicate: send service notices, security alerts and, where permitted, product updates you can opt out of.
  • Improve: analyse usage to fix issues and develop features.
  • Comply with law: meet tax, accounting, anti-money-laundering and legal-process obligations.

04Legal bases (where applicable)

Where laws such as the GDPR apply, we rely on: contract (to deliver the services you request), legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, product improvement), legal obligation (tax and compliance) and consent (for non-essential cookies and certain marketing). You may withdraw consent at any time.

05How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as follows:

  • Other users: profile information and content you choose to make public, and necessary details when you engage with a counterparty.
  • Service providers: hosting, payment processing, identity verification, analytics, email delivery and customer support, bound by contract to protect your data.
  • Legal and safety: where required by law or to protect rights, safety and the integrity of the Platform.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, with notice where required.

06International transfers

Marketsphare serves users worldwide, so your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses to protect transferred data.

07Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described above. Account data is generally retained while your account is active and for a limited period afterwards. Transaction and tax records are kept as long as law requires. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

08Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • delete your data, subject to legal retention duties;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, including direct marketing;
  • data portability, where processing is based on consent or contract;
  • withdraw consent at any time and lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise these rights, email privacy@marketsphare.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and we aim to respond within 30 days.

09Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar tools to run and improve the Platform. See our Cookie Policy for details and how to manage your preferences.

10Security

We use technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit, access controls and monitoring. No system is completely secure; see our Security page for more detail and how to report a vulnerability.

11Children's privacy

Marketsphare is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes in our practices or law. We will post the updated version with a new "last updated" date and, for material changes, notify you directly.
